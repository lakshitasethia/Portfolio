    A little about me

    Hey, I’m Lexi! I’m a third year CS student at DJSCE in Mumbai, studying Data Science, and I run on coffee, curiosity and the occasional hibernation arc.

    I started with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I still love making things look good. Then I got nosy about what’s happening underneath, so now I’m digging into backend, data structures and algorithms. Most of what I know comes from building things and breaking them (sometimes in that order, sometimes not). So far that’s a path tracer that lives in a single shader, hand gesture controllers, a 3D city you can walk around, and a tiny robotic arm that is my very first hardware project.

    My natural habitat is a hackathon at 3 AM with a cup of coffee going cold next to me. After that comes a full hibernation: blinds closed, phone off, no one allowed to talk to me until I’ve recovered.
